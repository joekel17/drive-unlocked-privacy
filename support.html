Delete Your Drive Unlocked Account

You can request deletion of your Drive Unlocked online account and its associated data at any time.

To request account deletion, email:

Request Account Deletion

Include your Drive Unlocked username. We may ask for additional verification to confirm account ownership. A username alone does not prove ownership.

Once ownership is verified, we will process deletion of your online account, username, XP and Barn Find leaderboard records. Any information we must retain for legal or security reasons will be explained to you.

Saved drives and route history stored locally on your device can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Drive Unlocked.

Location & Drive Recording

Drive Unlocked uses your device's location while an active drive is being recorded. Location is used to record your route, calculate distance and identify roads you have travelled.

If a drive is not recording correctly, check that location permission is enabled for Drive Unlocked in your device settings.

Saved Drives & Progress

Saved drives and route information are stored locally on your device. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove this information.

If you join the global leaderboards, your username, Explorer XP and Barn Find collection statistics are also stored online to support worldwide rankings.
